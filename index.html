TaC9 Server Sync — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy covers TaC9 Server Sync, operated by TaC9 PC Optimizations. It describes the sync bot, not the TaC9 PC software or Ticket Tool.

Purpose and scope

The bot copies approved information posts and later edits from selected public channels in this main server’s PC Services and TaC9 Optimization Suite sections to explicitly configured follower servers. It also maintains the approved category/channel structure and scoped permissions. It is not a general member-chat archive.

Only posts from the currently approved owner account are eligible for copying. Member/vouch copying is disabled. Open-ticket messages, private tickets, transcripts, and local suite chat are excluded from message copying. The policy channel itself is outside the PC Services sync allowlist.

Data processed

For synchronization, the bot processes eligible message text, embeds, attachments, message/author/channel/server identifiers, timestamps, and relevant channel, role, and permission metadata. It uses Message Content access to read ordinary posts and edits and recover missed updates from message history.

Discord can deliver events from other channels the bot can access. The software filters which messages it copies; received message objects may remain temporarily in the bot library’s memory cache until eviction or restart. Do not post passwords, access tokens, or sensitive personal information in channels selected for copying.

Who receives copied posts

Copies are posted inside the configured follower servers and are visible according to each destination channel’s permissions. The bot disables mention pings on its copied posts and edits.

TaC9 Server Sync — Data Handling & Requests

Hosting and storage

The bot runs on Railway. Message text, embeds, and downloaded attachment bytes are processed in memory to deliver approved copies. The bot is not designed to keep a persistent archive of raw message text or attachment files outside Discord.

Persistent bot storage keeps source/destination IDs, channel mappings, sync cursors, and content/attachment fingerprints so it can avoid duplicates and update the correct copy. Configuration, backups, reports, and logs may contain server/channel/role metadata, permissions, identifiers, delivery status, and diagnostics. Discord hosts the original and copied posts; Railway hosts the bot and its operational storage.

Retention

There is no automatic expiry for stored synchronization records, reports, or backups; they remain until manually removed. Temporary caches are cleared by eviction or restart. Copied Discord posts remain until removed in Discord. Deleting an original post does not automatically delete every follower copy.

Choices and privacy requests

There is no self-service user opt-out or deletion command. Ordinary members’ posts are currently ineligible for copying. The operator can change approved authors/channels/servers; follower administrators can remove the bot or revoke its channel access.

For a privacy question, report, correction, or deletion request, contact tac_9_fps in this main TaC9 server. Include relevant message/channel links and your request; never send passwords or tokens. Requests are reviewed manually and may need ownership verification. Removing bot records does not remove Discord messages.

Use of data and changes

The bot does not sell message data, profile members, or use message content to train AI or machine-learning models. Data is used for the described synchronization and operation. Material changes will be reflected in this channel with an updated date.
